The 'Old Natchez Trace' consortium (№ 010) traces America's earliest inland trade corridor—the 440-mile path from the Gulf port of Natchez to Nashville. The narrative theme 'From Gulf Port to Highland Trail' follows the Kaintuck boatmen who floated goods down the Mississippi and walked home through wilderness, where French, Spanish, and Native American foodways converged.

The 5-pepper lineup is:
- **Tabasco** (Anchor) — Louisiana, 30,000–50,000 SHU, The Bayou Foundation
- **Pequin** — Mexico → Texas, 40,000–60,000 SHU, The Wild Native
- **Hatch Green Chili** — New Mexico, 1,000–8,000 SHU, The Borderland Crossing
- **Red Jalapeño** — Mexico → Texas, 2,500–8,000 SHU, The Trail's Companion
- **Datil** — St. Augustine, Florida, 100,000–300,000 SHU, The Spanish Colonial Legacy
