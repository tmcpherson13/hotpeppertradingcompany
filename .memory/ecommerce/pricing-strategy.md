# Memory: ecommerce/pricing-strategy
Updated: 2026-01-10

## Weight Standardization

All products use a standard 2 oz (56.70g) bottle format:
- **Individual Cultivars**: 2 oz (56.70g)
- **Consortium Journeys**: 2 oz (56.70g)
- **Regional Consortiums**: 2 oz (56.70g)

## Individual Cultivar Pricing ($9-$15)

Pricing is based on market scarcity using a 4-tier system:

| Tier | Price | Description | Examples |
|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Common | $9 | Widely available cultivars | Cayenne, Ghost, Jalapeño, Poblano, Serrano, Habanero (Red) |
| Moderate | $11 | Moderately available | Fresno, Hatch Chile, Thai Bird's Eye, Scotch Bonnets, Tabasco, Cascabel, Guajillo, Ancho, De Árbol, Orange Habanero |
| Uncommon | $13 | Less common varieties | Chipotle Morita, Datil, Wiri Wiri, Peri Peri, Calabrian, Aleppo, Urfa Biber, Gochugaru, Pequin, Chiltepin |
| Rare | $15 | Scarce/specialty cultivars | Aji Limon, Aji Amarillo, Aji Panca, Aji Charapita, Devil's Breath, Red Savina Habanero, Rocoto, Trinidad Scorpion, Fatalii |

Note: Ghost peppers are categorized as "Common" due to widespread commercial availability despite their extreme heat level.

Pricing is based on heat intensity using a 4-tier system:

| Tier | Price | Heat Level | Consortiums |
|------|-------|------------|-------------|
| 1 | $17.00 | Mild-Moderate | Phoenician Legacy, Silk & Jade Passages |
| 2 | $19.00 | Moderate-Hot | Southern Crucible, Andean Diaspora, Manila Galleon |

## Pricing Philosophy

- Cultivar pricing emphasizes accessibility with an entry point at $9
- Consortium pricing reflects both curation value and heat intensity
- Letter of Marque commands the premium price as the highest-heat collection
- All prices display with decimals ($XX.00) for consistency
